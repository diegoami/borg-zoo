- **With the named list off, characters reach almost one character level higher and bounce between levels about a third as often.**
  - They kill 3 to 4 times as many breeders.
  - Deaths rise from 9 to 11 (shipped) and 14, but neither difference is significant at this size.
- **The extra deaths are early casters killed by monsters on the list.**
  - Necromancers die 0 times in ctl and fight, and 4 times in each list-off arm. All four die at CL1, before game turn 9,700: three to Grip, Farmer Maggot's Dog (on the list up to CL5), and one to a battle-scarred veteran in town (on the list up to CL8).
  - Priests die 1, 0, 3 and 3 times: at CL1-2, to a wild cat, a wild dog and Fang.
  - Mages go the other way: 2 deaths with the list (Grip, Fang), 1 without it in nolist, and 0 in both.
- **The breeder stand alone (`fight`) changes 38 of 90 games but not the outcome:** 1 rescued, 1 newly killed.
- Grip and Fang are the commonest killers in every arm.

The pattern is the pilot's: the list costs levels and creates level bouncing, but it keeps level-1 Necromancers and Priests away from the dogs. #505 (a danger-based scary rule instead of names) is where this would be addressed.
